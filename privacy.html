WorkoutX

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short: WorkoutX has no servers, no account, no ads and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Your data stays on your device

WorkoutX has no servers. Your profile, goal, workout history, schedules, meal plans, sleep plans and progress photos are stored only in the app’s private storage on your phone. The app doesn’t send this data over the internet, so nothing is uploaded, shared or sold, and there are no ads or analytics.

What the app collects

Only what you type in or choose: your name, gender, date of birth, weight, height, workout time, workout and meal choices, sleep times and progress photos. The developer never receives any of it.

No account or sign-in

WorkoutX doesn’t ask you to create an account or sign in. Anyone who can unlock your phone and open the app can see what is stored in it, so keep your phone secure.

Health data

If you allow it, WorkoutX reads your daily steps, calories burned and sleep from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android) to show your progress and recommend a program. It never writes to your health data, never shares it, and never uses it for advertising. You can turn access off at any time in your phone’s settings.

Camera, photos and notifications

The camera and photo library are used only when you add a progress photo. Photos are kept in the app’s private storage. Notifications are used only for reminders you set: workouts, meals, bedtime, wake-up alarms and monthly photo reminders.

App Store and Google Play

Apple and Google provide the store, downloads and the optional rating prompt. Their own privacy policies cover any data they collect when you use their services.

Children

WorkoutX is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Because all data stays on the device, the developer holds no data to delete; you can remove it yourself as described below.

Deleting your data

Profile → Reset App Data removes everything the app holds, including your profile and progress photos. Uninstalling the app does the same.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page and in an app update.
