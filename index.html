Do I need an account?

No. WorkoutX works without sign-up. Your profile, workouts, meals, sleep and progress photos are stored only on your phone.

Is WorkoutX free?

Yes. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases or ads.

Steps, calories or sleep show “—”

WorkoutX reads these from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android). On iPhone open Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → WorkoutX and turn the categories on. On Android open Health Connect → App permissions → WorkoutX.

Reminders don’t arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for WorkoutX in your phone’s settings and that Pop-up Notification is on in the Profile screen. On Android, battery saver can delay reminders.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Data stays on the device and isn’t synced, so it can’t be moved yet. Your phone’s full device backup (iCloud or Google) includes it.

How do I delete my data?

Profile → Reset App Data deletes everything, including progress photos. Uninstalling the app does the same.
